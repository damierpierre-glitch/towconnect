# Product Overview

- **Owner:** Founder / Product
- **Status:** Active
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-02
- **Review cycle:** Every phase
- **Related systems:** requests, dispatch, payments

## What TowConnect is

On-demand roadside assistance and towing. A motorist describes the problem,
sees a price **before** confirming, and is matched to an available operator by
Smart Dispatch rather than by phoning around.

## Where it operates

**Montréal and the South Shore.** Not "all of Canada" — the marketing copy was
corrected in the Phase 5 brand refresh precisely because an unproven coverage
claim is a promise the product cannot keep.

## The three people it serves

| Who | What they need |
| --- | --- |
| The motorist | Help quickly, a price they agreed to, and to know what is happening |
| The tow operator | Work they can accept or decline, and to be paid correctly |
| TowConnect operations | To see what needs a human, and nothing else |

## The journey

1. Describe the problem and the location.
2. See the estimated price. **Nothing is charged yet.**
3. Confirm — the card is *authorized*, not captured.
4. Smart Dispatch offers the job to one driver at a time, 18 seconds each.
5. Track the rescue; optionally share a **Safety Link** with somebody.
6. The driver progresses: en route → arrived → in progress → completed.
7. The payment is captured on completion. A receipt is available.

## What is deliberately not built

Subscriptions, loyalty, referral, national rollout, AI dispatch, AI fraud
detection. Stripe is in **sandbox**: no real money has moved.

**No commission rate has been decided.** The platform can compute, freeze and
pay compensation — it does none of it, because that decision has not been made.
